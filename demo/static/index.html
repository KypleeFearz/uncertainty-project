Uncertain or Genuinely Debatable?

Give the system a sentence and a statement. It decides whether to answer, to flag its own uncertainty, to show several reasonable readings or to ask back. The decision comes from an assessor that was frozen before testing and evaluated on held-out data.

Scope

English sentence pairs only. The assessor was trained on MNLI (ChaosNLI, 100 human votes per pair); on SNLI image captions the primary learned disagreement signal and response policy performed worse. Transfer varies by component. Disagreement is normalised entropy, not a probability; error risk and cues are estimates, not calibrated guarantees. Human votes are label judgements, not votes on which response the interface should show.
